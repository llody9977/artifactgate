# Contributing

Issues and focused pull requests are welcome. Please explain the threat, trust boundary
or operational problem being addressed and include tests for policy or decision-logic
changes.

Keep workflows least-privileged, pin third-party Actions to full commit SHAs, pin tool
versions, and fail closed when required evidence is unavailable. Changes under
`.github/workflows/`, `policy/` and `iac/` require maintainer review.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under Apache License 2.0.
Do not include private scan results, credentials or third-party material without a
compatible licence and attribution.
